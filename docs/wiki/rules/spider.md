# Spider

## Goal

Assemble eight complete same-suit runs from King down to Ace and move them to the foundations.

## Layout

- **Columns:** 10 columns: the first 4 hold 6 cards, the other 6 hold 5 (54 in total), top cards face up.
- **Stock:** 50 cards.
- **Foundations:** 8 foundations.

## Moves

- **Building:** Descending by rank, any suit.
- **Moving groups:** Only a descending same-suit run may move as a group.
- **Empty columns:** An empty column takes any card or run.
- **Completing a run:** A complete same-suit King-to-Ace run on top of a column moves automatically to the first free foundation; then the card beneath flips. This also applies to a run completed by a stock deal.
- **Foundations:** No moves from foundations.

## Stock

- Deals one face-up card onto each of the 10 columns.
- Not allowed while any column is empty.
- Not allowed when the stock is empty.

## Winning

8 complete runs on the foundations.

## Variants

104 cards:
- **1 suit:** 8 × spades.
- **2 suits:** 4 × spades and 4 × hearts.
- **4 suits:** 2 full decks.

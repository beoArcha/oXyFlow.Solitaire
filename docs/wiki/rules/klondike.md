# Klondike

## Goal

Move all 52 cards to the four foundations.

## Layout

- 52 cards.
- 7 tableau columns: column *i* holds *i*+1 cards (from 1 card in the first column up to 7 cards in the seventh), with only the top card face up.
- The other 24 form the stock.
- 4 foundations.
- A waste pile for cards drawn from the stock.

## Moves

- **Tableau:** Descending, alternating colors. Any correctly ordered run of face-up cards may move. An empty column takes only a king (or a run starting with one).
- **Foundations:** By suit, ascending from ace to king. An ace goes to any empty foundation.
- **Allowed moves:**
  - Waste to tableau or foundation.
  - Tableau to foundation.
  - Foundation to tableau (one card).
- **Waste:** Only the top waste card is playable, also in Draw 3.
- **Revealing cards:** After cards leave a column, a face-down card on top flips automatically.

## Stock

- Draws 1 or 3 cards to the waste (fewer if fewer remain).
- With an empty stock and a non-empty waste, it turns the waste back into the stock in the original order; passes are unlimited.
- When both the stock and the waste are empty, no cards can be drawn.

## Winning

52 cards on the foundations.

## Variants

- **Draw 1:** Draws 1 card from the stock to the waste.
- **Draw 3:** Draws 3 cards from the stock to the waste (fewer if fewer remain). Only the top waste card is playable.
